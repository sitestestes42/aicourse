The problem

AI can build a page in thirty seconds. It cannot tell you if the page is any good.

Most people type one big request, accept what comes back and hope. Then the menu overlaps the logo on a phone, the text is hard to read, the form sends nowhere and the design looks like every other template on the internet.

  • One-shot prompts that produce shallow, fragile sites
  • Code nobody understands and nobody can fix
  • Pages that fail on mobile, with a keyboard or in search
  • Generic design and copy full of clichés and invented claims
The solution

A repeatable workflow where AI does the typing and you keep the thinking.

Plan before you prompt

Define the audience, goal, sitemap and content first, so every prompt is specific and every result is checkable.

Build in small stages

Generate one section at a time, inspect it, and fix it with precise follow-up prompts instead of rebuilding from scratch.

Verify like a professional

Test responsiveness, accessibility, SEO and performance, then score the result on a 100-point audit.

No. The course starts from first principles and explains HTML, CSS and JavaScript in plain language. You will learn enough to read and judge what AI produces.

Which AI tool do I need?

The method works with any capable AI assistant. The prompts you build are tool-neutral, and you can copy them into whichever one you prefer.

Is it only watching videos?

No. Every lesson includes an interactive activity, a knowledge check, a practice exercise and an optional challenge. A lesson is only complete when you have done the work.

How long does it take?

Most lessons take 5 to 10 minutes, plus practice. Work at your own pace. Your progress is saved in your browser.

A complete coffee and bakery website called NOVA, step by step, and then a website of your own choice as your capstone project.
